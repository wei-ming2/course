### 2. Execution Best Practices
> Execution is never perfect from the get-go - always measure, test, and iterate

- You should be able to explain the 'why' behind every messaging that goes out to users
	- **Play store**: give smartphone more functionality
	- **Find my device**: fear of device theft

- **Note**: *Distribution channel* is as important as the *marketing message*
	- Youtube can be a support channel that is scalable
	- Context: salesperson lack the knowledge to address the problem, service center wait times can stretch hours

- **High-level steps to measure impact of execution**:
	1. Align on measurement metrics
	2. Decide on type of measurement test
	3. Run controlled, small-scale pilots if possible
	4. Learn, iterate, and scale

### 3. Nailing Consumer Research
| Type         | Explanation                                                                        |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Qualitative  | tease out specific user insights <br><br>Eg: 1-on-1 interviews                     |
| Quantitative | test the prevalence of user insights<br><br>Eg: mass market-representative surveys |

- Align on research parameter before kicking off:
	- **Methodology**: interview, survey
	- **Fieldwork**: interview length, interview dates
	- **Participants**: age, identity, gender, household income
	- **Quotas**: participants recruitment criteria, opportunity segment
![[Screenshot 2026-10-18 at 12.08.42 PM.png|300]]

#### 3.1. Qualitative Research
- **Tips**:
	- **'Peeling the onion'**: start with broad, expansive questions, then dig deeper on what they are telling you
	- **Taking copious notes**: apart from what was said, record down their raw emotions / words
	- **Note nonverbal cues**: hesitation, eyes lighting up, discomfort, etc
	- **Be visual and hands-on where possible**: go through the pain point by taking out their phone; reaction to new website
	- **Ask for their suggestions on possible solutions**: pre-emptively gather ideas
	- **Clear takeaways**: have a TLDR for a long report
	- **Gather data from all sources**: facebook, reddit, forums

- **Find overlap with quantitative study**: focus on eg top 3 factors
