### 1. Intro: Be a thinking, not just a do-er
#### 1.1. Types of people
1. **The 'non-thinker'**: sit there and wait for more specific instructions
2. **The 'scrambled thinker'**: jump to listing every possible solution in your head; but what to focus on, and why?

3. **The 'methodical thinker'** (ideal): clear framework to tackle the problem
	- Ie: what has worked in the past / vs. competitors, running numbers on ROI for every $1 invested
	- Data, user research, and a predictive model to put together a clear, logical analysis on solutions

#### 1.2. CALP Framework
- Frameworks are **repeatable building blocks** for success

- **C** - Clarify & Contextualise the problem
- **A** - Analyse the drivers
- **L** - List solutions
- **P** - Prioritise the solutions

- **Case study**: how can we increase the number of smartphone users in India?

##### 1.2.1. C - Clarify & Contextualise
- Better understand what is going on right now
- Is it a genuine problem

- **Background**: 60% of handset users in India own a feature phone (vs ~30% for APAC) <br>![[Screenshot 2026-10-16 at 12.07.59 PM.png|500]]

##### 1.2.2. A- Analyse
- What are the levers we can pull to solve the problem?
- Which of the 'levers' are practical vs. irrelevant?
- Talk to experts, review past studies, conducting new studies

- **Speak to key stakeholders**:
	- OEM partners (Samsung, Oppo, HMD)
	- IN marketing, etc
	- Surveys and interview on individual and groups (with a mix of age, gender, diversity)

- **What (potential) barriers to smartphone adoption to address in India?** 
	1. Smartphone affordability
	2. Functional barriers
	3. Lack of network coverage
	4. Data affordability
	5. Other barriers (smartphone supply constraint)
![[Screenshot 2026-10-16 at 12.17.01 PM.png|500]]

##### 1.2.3. L - List Solutions
- Think of solutions to target the super-specific drivers

1. **Conduct user education**: scale access programs; leverage PR
2. **Make product changes**: simplify the user experience; data-enabled software update
3. **Support partners in marketing**: support OEM partners; value-add efforts with funding
4. **Offer device support**: provide accessories and warranties; scale YouTube to improve post-sale support
